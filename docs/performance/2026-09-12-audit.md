## Master prioritized findings (fixed scope after initial audit)

| ID / severity | Bottleneck and impact | Proposed smallest fix / expected improvement | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P1 — HIGH | Venue dashboard repeats the same access DB chain six times per request. Adds round trips and DB load on each dashboard refresh. | Share the access-read promise only inside this GET request, keyed to the exact client and user; keep permission checks at each service call. Six chains become one; verify isolation, denied permissions, errors and next-request revocation. | Medium: authorization scope must never survive a request or apply to mutations. |
| P2 — MEDIUM | Confirmed pickup waits for external providers after durable lead/inbox commit. Provider delays can add seconds to submission. | Use Next's supported response-lifetime callback to deliver alerts after acknowledgement; keep handoff and replay checks synchronous and do not claim provider acceptance before delivery. | Medium: callbacks must stay attached to response lifetime and replay must not resend. |
| P3 — MEDIUM | Document-wide recovery reacts to unrelated DOM updates. Blocked autoplay test produces 30 extra scans/play attempts. | Observe additions of actual feed video elements/subtrees; keep media-readiness and visible-page recovery. | Low/medium: verify dynamic feed insertion and Safari recovery. |
| P4 — INFORMATIONAL pending measurement | Invariant root HTML is repeatedly loaded/hashed/transformed at origin. Large strings consume CPU/memory even though public data is loaded separately. | Measure warm route work before deciding on a deployment-local artifact promise. Do not extend HTTP/data freshness. | Low if restricted to immutable production files; development must remain live and read failures retriable. |
| P5 — INFORMATIONAL / future scale | High bitrate media, large CSS/role shell, directory caps/city counting, bounded raw admin analytics and filtering of legacy Following feed. | Keep as follow-ups unless current measured impact justifies changes. No feature removal, speculative indexes, blanket memoization/caching, or infrastructure. | Large changes need separate evidence and design. |

No critical finding. Priority order: P1, P2, P3; P4 only if the origin timing confirms material avoidable work. Final full review will not trigger a new general optimization cycle.
